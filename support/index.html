JioTransit support

Updated 5 October 2026

Made by Sim Sheng Xue under the NimShade name. Email xuecoding@gmail.com with your phone model, OS version, app version and a short description of the problem. Do not send payment credentials, government identifiers or API keys.

Add a bus widget

Find your stop → tap the stop's down arrow → tap Bus stop details > → tap the widget icon at the top right. This selects the stop for existing widgets too. Add a widget separately through your iPhone Home Screen or Android launcher. Widgets share the selected stop. The OS controls background refresh; tap refresh when estimates are stale.

Journey reminders

Choose your get-off stop and allow the necessary location/notification permissions. Timings and trip progress are estimates and can change. GPS, internet, battery settings and OS background restrictions can affect alerts. Keep an eye on your stop; reminders supplement your own attention.

Restore ad removal

Open JioTransit → tap the settings button at the top right of the home screen → Ad free → Restore purchase. Use the store account that bought ad removal. Apple and Google purchases are separate. Contact support if restoration fails; do not buy again just to troubleshoot.

Data and credits

Independent app, not affiliated with or endorsed by LTA. Arrival estimates use LTA DataMall; maps use SLA OneMap. See the in-app credits.

JioTransit Beta

Help test JioTransit

The Singapore bus app that tells you when to get off. Free while it's in beta.

  1. Get TestFlight

    Apple's free app for trying apps before they launch.

    Get TestFlight

  2. Join the beta

    Opens your invite in TestFlight. Tap Accept, then Install.

    Join the iPhone beta

  3. You're in

    JioTransit appears on your home screen. Send feedback any time from TestFlight.

If TestFlight says this beta isn't open yet, try again tomorrow.

  1. Join the tester group

    Tap Join group, using the Google account on your phone.

    Join the tester group

  2. Install from Google Play

    Tap Become a tester, then download JioTransit.

    Install JioTransit

  3. You're in

    Please keep it installed for 14 days. That's what Google needs before JioTransit can launch.

Test JioTransit on Android

Thanks for helping! Google Play lets a new app launch only after 12 people test it for 14 days in a row. It takes two minutes. Do the steps in order, on your Android phone.

1Join the tester group

Open the group, sign in with the same Google account you use for the Play Store, and tap Join group.

Join the tester group

2Become a tester and install

Come back here after joining. Tap Become a tester, then download it on Google Play.

Become a tester

3Keep it installed for 14 days

Open it now and then, and leave it installed for two weeks. That's all Google needs.

“Not available” or no Become a tester button? You haven't joined the group yet, or Play is using a different Google account. Join in step 1 with the account shown in your Play Store, wait a minute, then try step 2 again.
